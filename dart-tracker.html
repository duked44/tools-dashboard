How to get a free DART key
  1. Open dart.developer.azure-api.net, tap Sign up, and confirm your email.
  2. Go to Products, pick GTFS Realtime, and tap Subscribe.
  3. Go to Profile, tap Show next to Primary key, and copy it into the box above.
  4. Go to APIs, then GTFS Realtime, then Vehicle Positions. Copy the Request URL into the URL box.
If the feed is blocked: free proxy
  1. Sign up at dash.cloudflare.com. It's free.
  2. Go to Workers & Pages, then Create, then Create Worker, then Deploy.
  3. Tap Edit code, replace everything with the code below, and tap Deploy.
  4. Paste the worker's …workers.dev address into Proxy URL above.

Your key and spot stay on this phone only. The track map and station run times come from DART's published schedule (). Countdowns are estimates.
