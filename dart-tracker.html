How to get the key (on your phone)
  1. Open dart.developer.azure-api.net and tap Sign up. Confirm your email.
  2. Open Products, pick the GTFS Realtime product, and subscribe.
  3. Open Profile, then Show next to the Primary key, and copy it into the box above.
  4. Open APIs, then GTFS Realtime, then the Vehicle Positions operation. Copy its Request URL into the URL box above.
If the feed is blocked: free proxy (Cloudflare Worker)
  1. On your phone, sign up at dash.cloudflare.com. It's free.
  2. Go to Workers & Pages, then Create, then Create Worker, then Deploy.
  3. Tap Edit code, delete everything, paste the code below, and tap Deploy.
  4. Copy the worker's address (…workers.dev) into the Proxy URL box above.

Your key stays in this phone's browser. It is never put in the page's code.
Track map and station run times come from DART's published schedule (version ). Train positions come from DART's live feed. Countdowns are estimates, based on where the train is right now and DART's scheduled run times from that point.
